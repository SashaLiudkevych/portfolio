ASTRA WEBSITE UPLOAD

Upload this entire folder, preserving its subfolders, to your website.
Open https://YOUR-DOMAIN/WHATEVER-FOLDER/astra.html

Required:
  astra.html
  Mattress_cooling_draco.glb
  libraries/ (all files and folders)

This is a static site. No Node.js, npm installation, build step, or CDN is needed on your web server.
The Three.js modules and Draco decoder are included locally. Use an HTTP/HTTPS website URL, not a file:// URL.

If your host requires MIME configuration:
  .js   = text/javascript
  .wasm = application/wasm
  .glb  = model/gltf-binary

The button plays and reverses the embedded animation. Drag to rotate, scroll/pinch to zoom.
